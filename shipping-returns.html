Shipping & Returns Policy

Clear rules, no fine print games. Here is exactly how delivery and returns work at Drift Store.

Delivery times

DestinationTime after dispatchTracking
Pakistan (major cities)7–12 working daysEmail + order status page
Pakistan (small towns)9–15 working daysEmail + order status page
International10–20 working daysEmail tracking link

All items are dispatched from our international warehouse. Orders placed during sale periods (11.11, Black Friday, Eid) can take 2–4 extra days because of courier volume.

Shipping charges

Shipping is calculated at checkout and shown in USD before you pay. There are no hidden handling fees. If a parcel is returned to us undelivered because of an incorrect address, we contact you to re-ship.

Returns — 7 day window

You can return an item if:

  • It arrived damaged or broken
  • You received the wrong product, size or colour
  • The item is significantly different from the listing photos

You cannot return if:

  • More than 7 days have passed since delivery
  • The item was used, washed or altered
  • It is an innerwear, cosmetic or hygiene product that has been opened

How to start a return

  1. Email azaanbk29@gmail.com with your order number (starts with DR-).
  2. Attach 2–3 clear photos showing the issue.
  3. We confirm within one working day and tell you whether to return the item or keep it.
  4. Replacement or refund is processed within 5–7 working days after confirmation.

Refunds

Refunds go back the way you paid where possible: EasyPaisa, SadaPay or bank transfer for Pakistani customers, and your original card for international orders. Shipping fees are refunded only when the fault is ours.

Order cancellations

You can cancel free of charge any time before dispatch. Once tracking is issued, the order enters the normal return process instead.

Questions we get asked

How long does Drift delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 7 to 12 working days after dispatch. Items ship from our international warehouse, and a tracking number is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves.

Can I return an item?

Yes. Contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos if the item is damaged, wrong, or significantly different from the listing. We arrange a replacement or refund.

Who pays return shipping?

If the item arrived damaged or incorrect, we cover it. For change-of-mind returns, the customer covers return shipping.

Do you refund Cash on Delivery orders?

Yes. COD orders can be returned under the same 7-day rule; refunds are sent to your EasyPaisa, SadaPay or bank account.
