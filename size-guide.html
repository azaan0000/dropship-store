Size Guide — Measure Once, Order Right

Wrong size is the number one reason online clothing orders come back. Ten minutes with a measuring tape prevents it. Here is exactly how to measure, and how to pick between two sizes.

What you need

  • A soft measuring tape (or a string plus a ruler)
  • A mirror, or someone to help with shoulder width
  • Light clothing — not a thick sweater

How to measure

Chest

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, under the arms, keeping it level at the back. Arms relaxed at your sides. Note the number in inches or centimetres.

Shoulder width

Measure from the outer edge of one shoulder bone straight across the back to the other. This is the measurement that decides whether a jacket fits — fabric cannot be added to shoulders.

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your torso, usually just above the navel. Keep one finger under the tape so it is not tight.

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of the hips, feet together, tape level.

Sleeve length

From the shoulder bone to the wrist bone, with the arm slightly bent.

Between two sizes? Take the larger one. Loose can be worn or layered; tight gets returned.

Buying a winter jacket? Size up if you will wear a sweater under it. Check shoulder width first — that is the limit.

Knitwear and corduroy run slightly smaller than woven shirts. When in doubt, one size up.

Sizing questions

How do I measure my chest for a Drift top?

Measure around the fullest part of your chest with the tape parallel to the floor, arms down, over light clothing. Add 2 cm for a relaxed fit.

Should I size up for winter jackets?

Yes if you plan to wear a sweater underneath — go one size up. Jacket shoulders must match your shoulder width; you cannot tailor those.

What if my size is between two options?

Choose the larger one. Items that are slightly loose can be worn; items that are tight get returned.
