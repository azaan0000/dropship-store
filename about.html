About Drift Store

Drift Store is a curated online shop based in Punjab, Pakistan. We hand-pick trending products — winter fashion, home essentials, beauty tools and clever gadgets — and ship them directly to customers in Pakistan and worldwide.

How we choose products

Our catalogue is limited on purpose. Instead of listing millions of items, we test and curate a small number of finds with strong reviews and fair prices, so shoppers can decide quickly instead of scrolling forever.

How payment works

  • Pakistan: EasyPaisa or SadaPay mobile transfer, or Cash on Delivery where available.
  • International: a secure Payoneer card payment link emailed after you place the order (Visa/Mastercard accepted).

Every order is confirmed by email, and once it ships we send a tracking number you can follow on the order status page.

Delivery

Products are dispatched from our international warehouse and usually arrive in 7–12 working days. If anything goes wrong with a parcel, contact us and we will fix it.
