Imported winter items ki delivery Pakistan mein aam tor par 10–20 din leti hai. FAQ — Winter accessories Pakistan

Winter accessories Pakistan mein kitne ki start hoti hain?

Basic cozy home items (socks, slippers, blankets) $6–7 (≈ Rs 1,800–2,000) se shuru hoti hain. Proper heated jackets $28 (≈ Rs 8,000) tak. Poora winter budget 30–40k mein set ho jata hai.

Kya November mein order karna zaroori hai?

Haan — imported items ki delivery 10–20 din leti hai aur December mein popular sizes khatam ho jate hain. Late November = best value window.

Heated jacket kitni der chalti hai?

Aam tor par 4–8 ghante ek charge par, depending on heat level. USB power bank se chalti hai jo aap already use karte hain.

Winter items ki warranty hoti hai?

Blankets aur slippers ki nahi, lekin heated jackets aur electric items ki replacement policy hai — order par support se confirm karein.

Sizings kaise check karein?

Har product page par size chart hai. Imported sizes PK se 1 size bade assume karein (PK medium = imported large).
