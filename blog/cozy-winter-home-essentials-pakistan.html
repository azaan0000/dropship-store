Cozy Winter Home Essentials Pakistan — Feel-Fancy Setup Under $35

Pakistan mein jab November ki sardi shuru hoti hai to sab kuch ghar ke andar chala jata hai — chai, gupshup, Netflix aur long nights. Magar aksar ghar wahi sadha aur thanda feel hota hai. Sach yeh hai ke cozy ghar banane ke liye renovation ki zaroorat nahi — sirf 4 tarah ki chhoti cheezein chahiye, aur pura setup $35 se kam mein ho jata hai. Ye guide unhi items ki list hai, real prices ke sath.

Cozy home ka 4-point formula

Interior designers ka rule simple hai: winter coziness touch, light, warmth aur tidiness se aati hai. Aam tor par in par focus karein:

  • Soft textures: fleece, plush aur ribbed fabrics — towel, cushion, slippers. Bari carpet ka kaam chhoti items se nikal jata hai.
  • Warm lighting: ceiling ki white tube light band karke table ya wall lamp on karein. Warm glow se hi room ka mood 180 degree badal jata hai.
  • Floor warmth: thanda floor poore ghar ka temperature neeche kheenchta hai. Non-slip door mat aur thick-sole plush slippers iska sabse sasta fix hain.
  • Chupa hua clutter: sardi mein blankets aur sweaters har jagah bikhre hote hain. Ek compartmentalized storage box se folding drawer ban jati hai.
