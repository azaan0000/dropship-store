Winter Styling Tips — Pakistani Weather Ke Hisaab Se

1. Layering Karo — Lekin Smartly

Pakistan mein winter ka matlab extreme cold nahi hota (north ke ilawa). Isliye heavy coats ki jagah layering best hai. Ek knitwear top pehn ke us ke upar ek light jacket ya shawl — agar dhoop lag jaye to ek layer utaar do. Easy.

2. Colors — Winter Mein Dark Tones

Winter 2026 mein maroon, navy, olive green, aur charcoal grey sab se zyada chalne wale colors hain. Velvet suit in colors mein bohot classy lagta hai. White aur cream bhi winter looks mein viral ho rahe hain TikTok pe.

3. Shoes Ka Khayal Rakho

Winter mein sandals ki jagah closed shoes pehno. Boots agar mil jaye to best — otherwise sneakers bhi kaafi hain. Socks zaruri hain, especially agar aap northern areas mein ja rahe hain.

4. Accessories Se Look Complete Karo

Ek scarf, beanie, ya statement earrings se simple winter outfit bhi premium lagne lagta hai. Pakistan mein winter accessories locally mil jaate hain — kisi bhi bazaar se 200-300 rupees mein.
