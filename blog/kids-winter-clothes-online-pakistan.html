Order ka rule: pehle feet aur feet, phir layers

Bachon ka body heat bara logon se zyada tez kharaab hota hai, aur sabse pehle unke paaon aur sar thande hote hain. Isi liye kids ki winter shopping ka pehla kharcha socks, shoes aur boots par hona chahiye — jacket se pehle. Jacket to aap neeche layering ke sath manage kar sakte hain, lekin thande paaon raat bhar neend kharab karte hain aur sardi zyada jaldi bachon ko lagti hai.

Doosra rule: November mein order karo. Imported winter items ki delivery Pakistan mein 10 se 20 din leti hai. Agar aap December ke aakhri hafton mein order karenge to cheezein January ki thand mein aayengi jab bachon ko zaroorat kam ho rahi hogi. Online ordering ka sabse bara fayda ye hai ke aap ghar baithe poora stock compare kar sakte hain, aur imported items local bazaar se saste bhi milte hain.

Buyer ke liye 3 kaam ki tips

  • Size chart skip na karein: imported sizes Pakistani bazaar ke muqablay mein ek size chote hote hain. Bachon ki age aur height dono check karein, aur shak ho to ek size bara lein — bachon ke kapde ghar par "perfect fit" nahi, thora loose kharidne chahiye taake poora season chale.
  • Color selection: white aur light colors bachon par jaldi mail ho jate hain. Red, navy aur dark colors winter mein practical hain aur wedding-dawat ka kaam bhi de dete hain.
  • Ek saath order karein: shipping flat $4.99 hai aur $60 se upar FREE — poore bachon ke winter ka order ek cart mein dalein, shipping bach jayegi aur sab ek sath aayega.
