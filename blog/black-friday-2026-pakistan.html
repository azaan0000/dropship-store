Black Friday 2026 Pakistan — Online Shopping Guide & Early Deals

Black Friday 2026 ki tareekh: 27 November (Friday). Cyber Monday 30 November ko aayega, lekin Pakistan mein asli sale 20 November se hi shuru ho jati hai — Daraz 11.11 se hi discounts chalata hai. Is guide mein: dates, fake discount se bachne ka tarika, aur hamari taraf se hand-picked early deals.

Pro tip: 27 Nov ki raat tak intezar na karein — Pakistan mein shipping 5-12 din leti hai, isliye 20-24 Nov mein order karne par item December ke pehle hafte mein pohanchta hai.

Fake discount kaise pakrein — 3-step check

  1. Wishlist trick: Jo item chahiye, 2-3 hafte pehle wishlist mein daal dein. Black Friday par wohi price compare karein. Aksar sellers pehle price barha kar phir '50% off' lagate hain.
  2. Cross-store check: Daraz aur local page ka final price (delivery ke sath) compare karein. Kabe-kabe local page par 'sasta' item asal mein zaroori delivery fee ke baad mehnga hota hai.
  3. Review history dekhein: Item ke reviews 6+ mahine purane hon chahiye. Naye listings par 'flash sale' aksar clear karne ka tarika hota hai.

Black Friday mein kya khareedna behtar hai (aur kya nahi)

  • Worth it: Winter jackets/coats, blanket-type home items, beauty sets, aur gifting ke liye perfume/watches — seasonal items jin ki demand peak ke baad girti hai, unhi par asli discount hoti hai.
  • Avoid: Phones/laptops par 'Black Friday exclusive' prices — PK market mein international warranty aur custom ka masla hota hai; bharosemand seller se hi lein.

Common sawalat

Black Friday 2026 kab hai Pakistan mein?

Black Friday 27 November 2026 (Friday) ko hai. Cyber Monday 30 November, haalaanke aksar online stores 20 November se deals shuru kar deti hain aur pehli December tak chalati hain.

Kya Pakistan mein Black Friday deals sach mein hoti hain?

Ji haan — Daraz, Foodpanda aur cori dukaanein 11.11 se Black Friday tak barhi sales chalati hain. Lekin har '70% off' sach nahi hota: asli discount tab hai jab final price pichle mahine ke price se kam ho.
