"Gift jaisi" cheez ki pehchan

Ek gift 3 cheezon se banti hai:

  1. Packaging — karton ka quality, inner tray, colors. Imported items mein yeh aam tor par local se acha hota hai.
  2. Use-frequency — roz use hone wali cheez (sweatshirt, tissue box) har din yaad dilati hai.
  3. Story — "ye China se laye hain aap ke sard mein ke liye" wali factor. Imported gift ka apna charm hai.

Waqfa (timeline) aur delivery

Common sawalat

Under $30 (Rs ~8,000) mein acchi gift mil jati hai?

Ji haan — agar aap 'small but useful' soch ke chadein. Advent calendar, ornaments box, tissue holder jaise imported items 100% gift-worthy lagte hain aur packaging bhi achi hoti hai.

Kya ye gifts gift-wrap ke sath aate hain?

Independent wrapping nahi hoti, lekin imported packaging premium karton mein hoti hai. Aap chahein to order note mein 'gift' likh dein — packing aisi ki jati hai ke direct gift diya ja sake.

Pakistan mein shipping kitni leti hai?

7–12 working days, EasyPaisa/SadaPay ya COD checkout ke sath. November mein order karna behtar hai — December mein winter + holiday rush se 2–3 din aur lag sakte hain.
