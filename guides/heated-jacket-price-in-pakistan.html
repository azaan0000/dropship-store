Heated jacket hoti kya hai?

Jacket ke andar chest, back aur kabhi kabhi collar mein carbon fiber heating panels hote hain. Ek USB power bank in panels ko garam karta hai, aur jacket ke andar ka thanda hua area 3-5 minute mein garam ho jata hai. Temperatures aam tor par 35°C se 50°C tak jate hain, teen levels par — low, medium, high. Jacket machine washable hoti hai (panels waterproof hote hain) lekin wash se pehle power bank nikalna hota hai.

Buyer tips (zyada log yahan ghalti karte hain)

  • Power bank ka khayal: jacket ke sath power bank shamil nahi hota. 10,000 mAh ka bank high level par 4-5 ghante, low level par 8+ ghante chalata hai.
  • Size ek bara: heated jackets imported sizing mein aap ke PK size se ek size bare hote hain — size chart dekh kar order karein, aur shak ho to bara lein.
  • Barish mein hoshiyar: panels waterproof hote hain lekin zyada barish mein jacket ko panno se soaked na karein.
  • Timing: November mein order karo — December mein delivery aati hai jab sardi asli hoti hai, aur sizes khatam hone se pehle haath mil jata hai.
